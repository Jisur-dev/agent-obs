### A new way to understand software.


Modern software is becoming increasingly complex.

Distributed systems, AI agents, cloud infrastructure, autonomous workflows, and rapidly changing codebases make it difficult to answer a simple question:

> **What actually happened inside my system?**

jisur-agent-obs is an open-source platform built around a simple vision:

## **Make software completely observable.**

Not just its logs.
Not just its metrics.
Not just its traces.

**The whole story.**

From the moment something is triggered, jisur-agent-obs aims to make it possible to understand what happened, how different parts of the system interacted, what influenced the outcome, and how the system changed over time.

---

## One place to understand your software

jisur-agent-obs aims to connect the different pieces of engineering reality into one coherent view:

**Code → Execution → Behavior → Outcome**

Instead of investigating isolated logs, dashboards, traces, configurations, deployments, or AI interactions, the goal is to see them as parts of the same system.

The result is a deeper understanding of software behavior.

---

## Built for the software of today

jisur-agent-obs is designed with modern engineering in mind:

* Distributed applications
* Cloud-native systems
* AI and agentic systems
* Automated workflows
* Microservices
* Data pipelines
* Developer infrastructure
* Complex production environments

The long-term goal is not to build another monitoring dashboard.

It is to build a **common observability layer for software itself**.

---

## From observing to understanding

Traditional observability helps answer:

> **What happened?**

jisur-agent-obs aims to go further:

> **Why did it happen?**
> **What influenced it?**
> **What changed?**
> **What will happen if we change it?**

This opens the door to a different kind of engineering workflow — one where systems can be investigated, compared, reproduced, evaluated, and continuously understood.

---

## An open platform

jisur-agent-obs is intentionally designed to grow.

The platform is expected to evolve through contributions from developers, researchers, engineers, and future contributors.

Different contributors can work on different layers while contributing to the same larger vision.

**One platform.
Many disciplines.
One goal: understanding software.**

---

## The Bigger Vision

jisur-agent-obs is not intended to be just another tracing tool, monitoring system, AI observability product, or debugging utility.

The ambition is much broader:

### **Build infrastructure that lets engineers understand software as a living system.**

And make that infrastructure **open, extensible, and accessible to everyone.**
